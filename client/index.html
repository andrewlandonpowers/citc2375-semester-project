List of games:

This is a list of my favorite steam games of all time, in no particular order.

Terraria

Action RPG

This game is one of the highest selling video games of all time, ranking at 7th place with 70 million sales. This is for good reason. The game is great, easily moddable, and is jam packed with countless items to try to find, craft, collect, and use in battle. I've still never collected the terraprisma, to this day.

My rating: 9.9/10

The Terraria Steam page.

Persona 5 Royal

Japanese Role Playing Game/JRPG

This game is long. Really long. But its jrpg gameplay is among the best of the series with how fun it is, and it's full of entertaining enemies to fight, bosses to defeat and more. Unfortunately, it's filled with countless uncomfortable topics, but even despite those topics, it still is a good game. Exploring Tokyo is fun, the dungeons are even more fun, and the fights are satisfying.

My rating: 9.7/10

The Persona 5 Royal Steam page.

Fields of Mistria

Farming Simulator

This is a farming simulator with an artstyle reminiscent of old 1990's anime. The reason this is one of my favorites isn't because of the farming sim gameplay, but purely for the dialogue within the game. The characters in the game have unique dialogue for nearly every action you can do, from donating objects for research, to fishing a lot, to even earning a lot of money. It's fun. Moreover, the characters are likeable, for the most part, and there's quite a lot of furniture, animals, monsters, and more to be seen.

My rating: 9.1/10

The Fields of Mistria Steam page.

Hollow Knight: Silksong

Metroidvania

A really fun 2d platformer/metroidvania, filled with a lot of fun bosses, fun characters, interesting dialogue and expansions to the story of Hollow Knight. It's rather difficult, but it introduces things in such a way that without realizing it, you get more skilled at playing the game as you go on, and without realizing it, you'll find yourself having a unique playstyle that you're skilled at.

My rating: 9.7/10

The Silksong Steam page.

The Binding of Isaac: Repentance

Roguelite

The final dlc of one of the most famous roguelites of all time. With over 700 items to earn, find, and collect, you can create weird and unusual runs each and every time you play. It's actually, really, genuinely fun.

My rating: 9.7/10
